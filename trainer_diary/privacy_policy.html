Privacy Notice Trainer Diary

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Overview

Trainer Diary respects your privacy. The app is designed to keep your information on your device and does not collect or transmit your personal or health data to the developer or third parties.

Trainer Diary may access certain health information through Android Health Connect when you grant the corresponding permissions. This information is used only to provide health-related features within the app.

The app does not upload or share this health information. Information obtained from Health Connect is stored and processed locally on your device.

Data Collection & Sharing

  • No personal data collection: Trainer Diary does not send names, emails, location, health information, or other personal identifiers to the developer or any third party.
  • Health data: When you grant Health Connect permissions, the app may read the health information necessary for its features. This information is processed and stored locally on your device.
  • No analytics or ads: The app does not include advertising SDKs or analytics tracking.
  • No third-party sharing: Your information is not transmitted to the developer, advertising networks, analytics services, or other third parties.
  • Local storage: Information created or obtained by the app remains on your device and is not uploaded to an external server.

Result: Your information stays on your device and is not collected for advertising, analytics, profiling, or sharing with third parties.

Permissions Used & Why

Trainer Diary requests only the permissions required for its features.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Purpose: To send notifications about upcoming training sessions that you schedule in the app.

Notifications can be controlled or disabled at any time in your Android system settings.

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Purpose: To automatically restore your reminder alarms after the device restarts, ensuring that your scheduled training alerts continue to work.

No personal or health data is accessed or transmitted during device startup. The app only restores previously configured reminders.

android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM

Purpose: To schedule precise reminders for upcoming training sessions so that notifications are delivered at the time selected by you.

Exact alarms are used only for user-initiated training reminders.

android.permission.health.READ_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED

Purpose: To read the total calories burned recorded in Android Health Connect. Trainer Diary uses this information to display and calculate relevant training and activity information within the app.

This permission allows the app to read the corresponding health information from Health Connect. The information is not transmitted to the developer or any third party and remains on your device.

android.permission.health.READ_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED

Purpose: To read active calories burned recorded in Android Health Connect. Trainer Diary uses this information for features related to physical activity and training.

The app reads this information only after you grant the permission. It is processed locally and is not uploaded, sold, or shared with the developer or third parties.

android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION

Purpose: To detect physical activity and count your steps. Trainer Diary uses this permission to provide step-counting and activity-related features within the app.

Activity recognition information is processed and stored locally on your device. It is not transmitted to the developer, uploaded to external servers, sold, or shared with third parties.

Health information privacy: Trainer Diary does not collect health data on external servers. Health information obtained through Health Connect, including calorie data, is stored and processed locally on the user's device.

You can manage or revoke Health Connect permissions at any time through Android system settings or Health Connect.

Children's Privacy

Trainer Diary is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect or transmit information from anyone, including children.

Security

Trainer Diary stores user-created information such as name, date of birth, gender, and training records locally on the device in a private database.

Health information obtained through Android Health Connect is also processed locally. The app does not transmit this information to the developer or third parties.

These records and health-related information do not leave your device through Trainer Diary.

Access to this information is protected by the security features of the Android operating system. Users can manage the app's permissions and Health Connect access directly in system settings.

Changes to This Notice

If the app's functionality changes in a way that affects privacy, data handling, or permissions, this notice will be updated accordingly.
