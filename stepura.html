Privacy Notice Stepura

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Overview

Stepura respects your privacy. The app is designed to keep your information on your device and does not collect or transmit your personal or health data to the developer or third parties.

Stepura may access certain health information through Android Health Connect when you grant the corresponding permissions. This information is used only to provide activity and step-counting features within the app.

The app does not upload or share this health information. Information obtained from Health Connect is stored and processed locally on your device.

Data Collection & Sharing

  • No personal data collection: Stepura does not send names, emails, location, health information, or other personal identifiers to the developer or any third party.
  • Health data: When you grant Health Connect permissions, the app may read the health information necessary for its activity-related features. This information is processed and stored locally on your device.
  • No analytics or ads: The app does not include advertising SDKs or analytics tracking.
  • No third-party sharing: Your information is not transmitted to the developer, advertising networks, analytics services, or other third parties.
  • Local storage: Information created or obtained by the app remains on your device and is not uploaded to an external server.

Result: Your information stays on your device and is not collected for advertising, analytics, profiling, or sharing with third parties.

Permissions Used & Why

Stepura requests only the permissions required for its activity and step-counting features.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Purpose: To display notifications related to step counting and activity tracking.

Notifications can be controlled or disabled at any time in your Android system settings.

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Purpose: To automatically restore the step-counting service after the device restarts so that step counting can continue to work.

No personal or health data is accessed or transmitted during device startup. The app only restores its step-counting functionality.

android.permission.health.READ_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED

Purpose: To read total calories burned recorded in Android Health Connect. Stepura uses this information to display activity statistics.

This permission allows the app to read the corresponding health information from Health Connect. The information is not transmitted to the developer or any third party and remains on your device.

android.permission.health.READ_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED

Purpose: To read active calories burned recorded in Android Health Connect. Stepura uses this information for activity statistics.

The app reads this information only after you grant the permission. It is processed locally and is not uploaded, sold, or shared with the developer or third parties.

android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION

Purpose: To detect physical activity and count your steps. Stepura uses this permission to provide step-counting and activity-related features within the app.

Activity recognition information is processed and stored locally on your device. It is not transmitted to the developer, uploaded to external servers, sold, or shared with third parties.

android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH

Purpose: To allow Stepura to run its step-counting service as a foreground service while tracking activity in the background.

The foreground service is used to maintain step counting while you are not actively using the app. Activity data processed by the service remains on your device.

Health information privacy: Stepura does not collect health data on external servers. Health information obtained through Health Connect, including calorie data, is stored and processed locally on the user's device.

You can manage or revoke Health Connect permissions at any time through Android system settings or Health Connect.

Children's Privacy

Stepura is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect or transmit information from anyone, including children.

Security

Stepura stores information required for step counting and activity statistics locally on the device.

Health information obtained through Android Health Connect is also processed locally. The app does not transmit this information to the developer or third parties.

This information does not leave your device through Stepura.

Access to this information is protected by the security features of the Android operating system. Users can manage the app's permissions and Health Connect access directly in system settings.

Changes to This Notice

If the app's functionality changes in a way that affects privacy, data handling, or permissions, this notice will be updated accordingly.
